26 to 28 Oct covers the country groups only; General group exports run to 25 Oct. Most of the last two days' activity was in #4 Germany (70), USA (55) and UK (36), and #3 Canada (12) and UK (11).

Posted by the amber Team

  • 23 Oct: Webinar topic survey re-sent with @all in all four communities. In #3 a member asked whether the form was real, and the team explained what it is for.
  • 27 Oct: In Community #3, the team told members asking for Amber Scholar updates that results will be announced by 31 January.
  • 21 to 27 Oct: Admins removed repeated unwanted posts from several accounts across groups.

Headline Takeaways

  • Community #4 carried almost all activity: 684 of 787 messages (87%). Its Germany group was the busiest single group with 275 messages.
  • Real peer guidance in #4: members explained Germany's 13-year education rule, Studienkolleg, the blocked account, living costs, Ausbildung and the Summer 2027 intake (portals close 15 Jan 2027).
  • Amber Scholar explained by a member: in #4 UK, a member walked others through the application (four categories, about 15 minutes, and you can apply before getting an offer letter). Questions about how winners are selected are still coming up.
  • Communities #1 and #2 were almost silent: 16 and 3 messages, with most country groups posting nothing.
  • IG Broadcast keeps growing: 841 members, up 49 since 26 Oct (792) and 390 since 6 Oct.
Needs Attention
  • Trust: in #3, a member asked if the webinar form was real, and members keep asking when Amber Scholar results come out (the team has now said by 31 January). In #1 UK, a reply to an Amber Scholar question said it was "for nothing". A short pinned post explaining Amber Scholar and the webinars would help.
  • Unsolicited posts: room-for-rent listings for Hull (twice) and Leeds (#1 UK) and a career-coaching promo (#3 UK).
  • Tone: a heated exchange between a few members in #4 Germany on 23 Oct; another member asked everyone to keep it respectful.
Possible lead
An agency account asked for accommodation in Liverpool in Community #2's UK group (23 Oct). Worth a direct follow-up.
